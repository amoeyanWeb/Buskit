About Me

The Fusion of Music, Sound and Electronics

For more than four decades, I have been immersed in the world of playing all kinds of musical instruments — percussion, melodic and harmonic — as well as technical electronics engineering. My focus has always been on creating tools that blur the line between technology and art.

Today, as a musician with a deep understanding of the shortcomings of electronic equipment with the unique capabilities needed for live performances — especially street musicians or small group rehearsals — I combined these three fields to create a collection of modular devices that address all the shortcomings of live performances, and I named this ecosystem Buskit.

Micro Preamp

Buskit microPA

This device is the smallest possible form of a compact preamp — low noise with high gain — through which acoustic instruments or microphones can be connected to sound systems.

Features

  • Analog Volume Control
  • Wireless Module Connectivity
  • Connection to AUX Port of Sound Systems
  • 6.3mm Input and Output
  • LED Low Battery Indicator
  • LED Power Indicator
  • LED with 9V Battery
  • Maximum 15dB Output Gain
  • On/Off Switch

Applications

  • Converting Acoustic Instrument to Electric
  • Outdoor Practice
  • Travel and Tour
  • Small Performances

Benefits

  • Small Dimensions
  • Low Weight
  • No Need to Drill the Instrument Body

Mini Mixer

Buskit Mini Mixer

This device is a powerful audio mixer with high quality and high gain that directly accepts three different audio sources, and with the help of the LineEXP module, its input count can be increased. It has a 5-band EQ and a rechargeable built-in battery.

Features

  • Equipped with Two 5-Band EQs with Independent Signal Volume Control
  • Low Band EQ: 120, 500, 1500, 3000, 6000 Hz
  • Two Piezo Inputs, One Line Input and One AUX Input
  • Gain and Drive Volume Control for Both Preamps
  • Amp and AUX Output
  • Backing Track Input Capability
  • Monitoring Capability Using Buskit Monitoring Module
  • Headphone Connection via Headphone Module
  • Dynamic or Condenser Microphone Connection (Using Microphone Module)
  • USB-C 5V/1A Charging

Applications

  • Use in Group Practice
  • Use in Street Performances

Benefits

  • Small and Lightweight Dimensions
  • Two Separate Preamp Inputs with EQ

Why Buskit

Real Problems, Practical Solutions

Every musician — whether for private or public, solo or group performances — needs equipment that provides the right facilities for their situation. The Buskit device collection meets all their needs effectively and efficiently.

⚠️

Existing Problems

  • Dependence on Mains Power or Heavy, Bulky Batteries
  • Limited Number of Amplifier Inputs
  • Live Performance for More Than Two People Requires Multiple Amplifiers
  • Need for an Audio Monitoring System
  • Need to Drill Instruments to Install Under-Bridge Microphones
  • Lack of an Integrated Ecosystem
  • Inadequate Audio Balance in Group Rehearsals
  • Need for EQ to Adjust Treble and Bass of Instrument or Vocalist
  • Ability to Connect Any Audio Equipment to the Performance
  • Inability to Use Amateur Amplifiers
✅

Buskit Ecosystem Solution

  • Offering Two Audio Mixer Models with Unique Capabilities For every taste and every need, a device has been designed to mix all audio input sources together without quantity limitations and produce a clear sound at the output.
  • Equipped with Rechargeable Built-in Battery — Most Buskit products are designed with USB-C rechargeable batteries; perform anywhere in the city without needing mains power.
  • Small, Pocket-Sized and Lightweight The entire multi-person system can be carried by hand and its weight barely reaches one kilogram.
  • Modular and Expandable Architecture Depending on needs, modules appropriate to the work level can be used, and when expanding, expansion modules can be purchased to develop the devices.
  • Equipped with Audio Monitoring System Audio monitoring capability plays a fundamental role in improving performance quality.
  • Equipped with Up to 8-Band Audio EQ A range of two-band, 5-band and 8-band equalizers are available in the Buskit device collection, playing a fundamental role in shaping the sound of an instrument or vocalist.
  • No Need to Drill Instruments or Install Under-Bridge Microphones
  • One Ecosystem, One Standard — All Buskit products are compatible with each other; from the smallest Mic Interface to the Mixer, they all speak the same language.
  • Using Amateur Amplifiers — Given the capabilities of the Buskit collection, connecting instruments to the AUX inputs of amplifiers is also possible, so a large group can be set up using just one ordinary amateur amplifier.
  • Equipped with System to Connect to AUX Input of Any Sound System —This capability allows any type of audio device to be used to connect an instrument or the full group's sound output.
  • Equipped with System for Direct Recording of Mixed Output Audio on Mobile Phone
  • Equipped with High-Gain, Low-Noise Preamp
Buskit is the only brand in the world that has designed and registered a fully modular and battery-powered audio ecosystem specifically for street musicians.
